GCA grading scale

Understand the grade.

This sample scale is a starting point. GCA's official grading standards should be finalized before accepting submissions.

10GEM MINT

Exceptional overall condition.

9MINT

Nearly flawless with very minor issues.

8NM–MT

Very strong condition with slight wear.

7NEAR MINT

Light, visible signs of handling.

6EXCELLENT

Noticeable wear but well preserved.
